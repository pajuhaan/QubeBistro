# QubeBistro | The Robotic Dining Experience

QubeBistro is an innovative, fully automated, staff-less robotic restaurant designed to revolutionize the dining experience. By combining cutting-edge robotics and automation technology, QubeBistro provides a fast, efficient, and personalized food experience—all in a compact, modular cube.

## Overview

QubeBistro is a fully automated restaurant housed in a cubic structure. The system allows customers to customize their food and order through an intuitive touchscreen or app, with ingredients directly sourced from local suppliers. The restaurant requires minimal space, reduces food waste, and eliminates traditional supply chains, delivering fresh food directly to consumers in the most efficient way possible.

## Features

- **Compact and Modular Design**: The Cube is designed to be easily installed in a variety of locations, including cities, offices, and even homes.
- **Automation & Robotics**: All food preparation and service are fully automated, ensuring speed, consistency, and accuracy in every order.
- **Personalized Orders**: Customers can personalize their meals based on dietary preferences, making it easy to choose ingredients and portion sizes.
- **Smart Lock Boxes**: Food is delivered in temperature-controlled lock boxes that are either warm or cold, with clear, color-coded indicators to show when the food is ready.
- **Efficient Supply Chain**: Ingredients come in cartridge-like boxes that are easily loaded by suppliers, reducing waste and eliminating the need for traditional grocery supply chains.
- **App Integration**: Customers can place orders in advance via an intuitive mobile app, track their food’s progress, and unlock the lock boxes once their meal is ready.

## How It Works

1. **Order Placement**: Customers place their order via the CubeBistro touchscreen or mobile app.
2. **Ingredient Loading**: Local suppliers deliver fresh ingredients in special cartridge-like boxes to the CubeBistro.
3. **Food Preparation**: The robotic system inside the CubeBistro prepares the meal based on the customer’s order.
4. **Lock Box Delivery**: Once the food is ready, it’s placed in one of the temperature-controlled lock boxes.
5. **Customer Pickup**: The lock box turns a specific color (as shown in the app) and becomes transparent when the food is ready. The customer unlocks the box with the app and picks up their meal.

## Benefits

- **Space Efficiency**: QubeBistro requires only a fraction of the space of a traditional restaurant, making it suitable for locations with limited space.
- **Fresh & Sustainable**: By sourcing ingredients directly from local suppliers, QubeBistro ensures the use of fresh, high-quality produce while minimizing food waste.
- **Convenience**: With automated food preparation and easy-to-use technology, customers enjoy a fast, hassle-free dining experience.
- **Customization**: Each meal is personalized, allowing customers to control what goes into their dish, catering to various dietary needs and preferences.
- **Reduced Costs**: Eliminating the need for traditional staff, chefs, and supply chains reduces overhead costs and boosts efficiency.

## Installation

To install a QubeBistro unit, follow these simple steps:

1. **Location Selection**: Choose a suitable space for the QubeBistro unit, keeping in mind it requires minimal space but should be easily accessible to customers.
2. **Setup**: The QubeBistro unit is shipped pre-assembled and ready for installation. Connect to power, water, and internet sources for full functionality.
3. **Ingredient Supply**: Coordinate with local suppliers to ensure a steady supply of fresh ingredients in cartridge-like boxes.
