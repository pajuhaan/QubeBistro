The CubeBistro Experience

CubeBistro is built on one simple idea: simplicity in every bite. Our robotic kitchen inside a sleek, compact CubeBistro ensures that every meal is prepared and delivered with maximum efficiency. Let's take a look at what makes CubeBistro unique:

  • Compact, Smart Design: The CubeBistro restaurant is housed in a cube-shaped unit, a marvel of modern design that requires only a fraction of the space of traditional restaurants. This compact setup allows us to offer high-quality meals without the need for extensive physical infrastructure.
  • Automated Cooking Process: Once you place your order via the touchscreen interface or the CubeBistro app, the magic begins. Inside each CubeBistro, robotic arms and automated systems prepare your meal precisely according to your preferences. The process is efficient, precise, and completely automated, ensuring consistency in every dish.
  • Cartridge-Based Ingredients: We eliminate the need for traditional grocery stores and supply chains with our innovative ingredient cartridges. Ingredients come in specialized cartridge-like boxes that fit perfectly into the CubeBistro. These cartridges are directly supplied by farmers, ensuring a fresh and sustainable approach to food sourcing. No middlemen, just quality ingredients.
  • Temperature-Controlled Lock Boxes: Once your meal is ready, it’s securely stored in one of our temperature-controlled lock boxes. These smart boxes maintain the perfect temperature for your food – whether it's hot or cold – until you're ready to pick it up. When your food is ready, the box will glow in a color that matches the one shown on your CubeBistro app, guiding you to your meal.
  • Effortless Ordering and Pickup: With CubeBistro, you have full control over your dining experience. Whether you're ordering in advance or grabbing a quick bite on the go, our app and touchscreen ordering system make it easy. Simply tap your selection, track your meal’s progress, and unlock your lock box once it's ready.
